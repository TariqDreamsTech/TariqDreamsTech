In Plain English: How It Works

Think of this system as a private, 24/7 digital factory that lives on our own dedicated server. Instead of paying expensive monthly rental fees to commercial automation websites that charge for every single click and data transfer, we built our own private cloud engine.

Whenever a customer orders a pet portrait, a car dealership adds a new vehicle, or a lead fills out an inquiry form, our server immediately hears about it. It analyzes the information, creates beautiful social media posts or AI artwork, updates both sales systems (HubSpot and Zoho), and sends instant confirmations—all in about 13 seconds without any human needing to touch a keyboard.

Technical Deep Dive: 4-Tier Engineering

  • Dedicated Linux VPS & Coolify PaaS: Hosted on a high-throughput Linux server (95.217.22.200:8000) managed via Coolify v4.0.0 container orchestrator. Isolated Docker networks ensure complete process security.
  • Stateful PostgreSQL Persistence: Production service n8n-with-postgresql maintains execution history, cached credentials, and transactional state with automated local snapshots and persistent volume mounts.
  • Enterprise Reverse Proxy & SSL: Traffic routes through production domain henry.lat backed by automated Let's Encrypt TLS/SSL certificates, webhook signature verification, and OAuth2.0 token refreshes.

Why Dedicated Hosting Beats Third-Party Clouds

Most businesses use shared cloud services where prices scale with every task executed. At 50+ workflows running thousands of times per day, third-party bills quickly reach thousands of dollars per month while imposing restrictive rate limits.

By self-hosting on our own private server with Coolify, we eliminated the monthly "cloud task tax" entirely. Our automations execute at lightning speed because our database and automation engine live on the same dedicated hardware, completely immune to external outages or API rate tier penalties.

Production Environment Configuration

The container stack is defined with strict security isolation, persistent volume mappings, and health checks to guarantee 99.9% operational uptime:

docker-compose.production.yml (Coolify PaaS) 
services:
  orchestrator:
    image: n8nio/n8n:latest
    restart: always
    environment:
      - DB_TYPE=postgresdb
      - DB_POSTGRESDB_HOST=postgres
      - DB_POSTGRESDB_DATABASE=n8n_prod_db
      - N8N_ENCRYPTION_KEY=${SECURE_VAULT_KEY}
      - WEBHOOK_URL=https://henry.lat/
      - EXECUTIONS_DATA_PRUNE=true
      - EXECUTIONS_DATA_MAX_AGE=168 # 7 days rolling
    volumes:
      - /data/coolify/orchestrator:/home/node/.n8n
  postgres:
    image: postgres:16-alpine
    volumes:
      - /data/coolify/postgres:/var/lib/postgresql/data

If Facebook or Telegram updates an API endpoint, our built-in error alert channels immediately notify us on Telegram with the exact step that needs attention, while the platform automatically retries the task up to 3 times with exponential backoff so no client data is ever lost.

Telemetry & Fault Tolerance Architecture

  • 13.33s Average Execution Latency: Highly optimized node sequences with in-memory JSON buffering and batch API queries reduce processing time from minutes down to 13 seconds.
  • Configurable Retry Mechanisms: Nodes interacting with external third-party APIs (Facebook Graph, Telegram, Google Gemini) feature automated 3-tier exponential backoff retries (1s, 5s, 15s).

Production Workflow: Pet Portrait Watercolor - Gemini AI PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE · 1280 × 720

In Plain English: Turning Pet Photos into Art

Customers upload a photo of their dog or cat through an online form. In the past, a graphic designer had to manually open the photo, mask out the messy background, apply digital paint brushes, and export the file.

Now, our Gemini Multimodal AI pipeline inspects the photo instantly. It recognizes the pet's breed, fur color, and lighting, removes background clutter, and prompts Gemini with specialized artistic watercolor parameters. Within seconds, a high-resolution watercolor portrait is rendered, watermarked, uploaded to cloud storage, and sent straight to the customer's inbox.

Gemini Multimodal Engineering Highlights

  • Binary Buffer Ingestion & Normalization: Image files (JPG, PNG, HEIC) are sanitized, stripped of sensitive EXIF metadata, and converted to Base64 buffers optimized for the Gemini vision endpoint.
  • Dynamic Watercolor Prompt Synthesis: The pipeline injects specialized styling directives: "vibrant translucent watercolor wash, soft pigment bleeding, splattered granulation on cold-press paper, crisp focal detail on eyes and nose".
  • Automated S3 CDN Storage & Signing: Synthesized assets are passed through a Sharp/Canvas node for subtle branding, saved to cloud storage, and assigned a time-limited signed download URL.

In Plain English: Hands-Free Car Sales Marketing

Car dealerships need to post newly arrived vehicles to Facebook pages every single day to attract local buyers. When done by hand, sales managers spend 2 to 3 hours daily typing out mileage, prices, financing terms, and uploading car photos.

Our automated social syndication engine continuously watches the dealership inventory. As soon as a car is ready for sale, it automatically writes an engaging sales post with verified vehicle specs, generates multi-photo carousels, and publishes directly to Facebook. When an interested buyer clicks or messages, their contact info is instantly added to both HubSpot and Zoho CRM for sales reps to call.

Facebook Graph API & Multi-CRM Technical Flow

  • Long-Lived Page Access Token Management: Configured OAuth2 refresh flows ensuring uninterrupted posting to multi-tenant Facebook pages without token expiration drops.
  • Inventory Deduplication in PostgreSQL: Stores VIN hashes in the dedicated database. If a vehicle has already been published in the last 7 days, redundant postings are automatically suppressed.
  • Dual CRM Schema Normalization: Translates Facebook instant lead form payloads into unified JSON objects dispatched in parallel to HubSpot CRM (Contacts & Deals) and Zoho CRM (Accounts & Leads).
