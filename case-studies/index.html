Adversarial Federated Learning · Safety-Critical Perception

Trust as a Gate, Not a Weight: Robust Federated Aggregation for Safety-Critical Perception

Lead author empirical research study evaluating Byzantine-robust federated learning on real-world autonomous driving perception (BDD100K dataset, 20 vehicle clients across 8 physical weather and lighting domains). Discovered that geometric aggregators (Krum) suffer from severe size bias — discarding 95% of training data and losing 9.2 balanced-accuracy points on clean data. Engineered Trust-Gated Aggregation (TAG), combining sample-count weighting with a size-invariant cosine alignment gate and median norm clipping — matching clean FedAvg (0.710) with 0.000 clean cost, remaining within 0.004 under all 6 attacks up to a 45% Byzantine fraction, and holding pedestrian miss rates within a rock-solid band of 0.012.

Cybersecurity · Network Intrusion Detection (NIDS)

ANOVA F-Test Feature Selection for Machine Learning-Based Network Intrusion Detection on UNSW-NB15

Lead author empirical research study designing a lightweight, high-performance two-stage Network Intrusion Detection System on the modern UNSW-NB15 benchmark (82,332 flow records). Applied an ANOVA F-statistic to compress 49 raw network flow attributes down to 15 top-ranked features (69.4% dimensional reduction) shared identically across binary and multi-class tasks. Attained 98.67% binary detection accuracy via variance-smoothed Gaussian Naive Bayes (surpassing Random Forest at 97.49% and SVM at 85.99%), achieved 78.13% multi-class attack identification via tuned XGBoost across 9 threat families, and diagnosed critical minority class imbalance bottlenecks (macro-F1 = 0.39).
