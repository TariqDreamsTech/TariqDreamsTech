PILLAR 9, 10 & 14: SANDBOXED DRIVE & SOVEREIGN KILL-SWITCH

Zero-Knowledge Sandboxed Drive & Emergency Data Corrupt Kill-Switch

Your data should never belong to big tech surveillance monopolies. Ranbval guarantees absolute mathematical ownership: media opens only within a sandboxed secure drive, supported by an irreversible emergency kill-switch.

  • View-Once Media Proof System: Sensitive documents and photos self-destruct after viewing, logging a cryptographic verification proof without leaking file bytes.
  • Sandboxed Drive Application: Confidential files (contracts, scans, private galleries) are encrypted with user-held KMS keys and can only be decrypted inside the isolated Ranbval enclave.
  • Emergency Data Corrupt Kill-Switch: Under physical coercion, device seizure, or legal breach, pressing the sovereign kill-switch permanently overwrites all master KMS keyrings and database shards with cryptographically random noise across all cloud clusters simultaneously.
  • Sovereign Data Export: 1-Click download of your complete account history, media, and encryption keys in open standard formats with zero vendor lock-in.
