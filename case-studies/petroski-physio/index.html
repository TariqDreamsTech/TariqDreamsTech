WORKFLOW EVOLUTION

The Manual Clinical Friction vs. The Deterministic Engine

How Petroski Physio eliminated messy cross-department communication, HIPAA privacy risks, and outdated marketing rosters.

The Old Limitation

Marketing Chasing Therapists in Clinic Hallways

Marketing team members constantly interrupted therapists between patient consultations asking which athletes were cleared for filming. Therapists were forced to open separate spreadsheets or log into marketing boards, adding cognitive overhead to busy patient treatment days.

Fragmented focus · Patient consultation interruptions
The Petroski Solution

1-Click Slack Direct Message via Automation Bot

24 hours after an athlete's evaluation, an automated bot sends a direct Slack notification to the assigned therapist with patient details and an interactive Yes/No approval button. The therapist makes a 1-second decision without leaving their daily communication hub.

1-click decision · Zero clinical disruption
The Old Limitation

Brittle Cross-Workspace Mirror Columns Failing in Zapier

Initial attempts linked boards using Monday.com mirror columns. However, mirror columns frequently failed to trigger webhooks, lagged during multi-item lookups, and blocked downstream Zapier automations, leading to missing patient records.

Unreliable sync · Webhooks failing silently
The Petroski Solution

Deterministic Direct Field Mapping at Item Creation

Replaced mirror columns entirely with direct field extraction (Name, Sport, Injury, Patient Status, Evaluation Date, Assigned Therapist) written directly into the Marketing board upon creation, ensuring 100% deterministic webhook delivery and execution.

100% reliable execution logs · Zero mirror column lag
The Old Limitation

Monday User IDs Mismatching Slack Workspace Identities

Monday.com internal numeric user IDs (e.g. User #8194) do not match Slack member IDs or external email handles. Without identity resolution, notifications had to be blasted into public broadcast channels where they were ignored.

Channel noise · Missed approval requests
The Petroski Solution

Dynamic Therapist Directory & Targeted Direct Messages

Integrated an auditable Therapist Directory in Google Sheets. The Zapier engine dynamically queries the spreadsheet to map the therapist's Monday ID to their verified Slack email, ensuring 100% accurate, private Direct Message delivery.

100% accurate private DM routing · Zero broadcast spam
The Old Limitation

Filming Discharged Patients Due to Stale Marketing Lists

When an athlete finished rehabilitation and was discharged, Marketing had no idea unless told manually. Videographers frequently scheduled filming sessions with patients who were no longer active clinic patients, causing embarrassment and scheduling waste.

Filming discharged patients · Outdated rosters
The Petroski Solution

Dynamic Lifecycle Re-Routing to "Former Filmable Athletes"

Whenever a patient's status changes to "Discharged" in the CRM, a Zapier webhook instantly intercepts the update and shifts the item from "Current Filmable Athletes" to "Former Filmable Athletes", preserving real-time operational roster hygiene.

Real-time discharge sync · 100% brand safety
CLINICAL GOVERNANCE & ARCHITECTURE

Two-Board Architectural Isolation & Zero Clinical Backflow

In medical and physical therapy environments, clinical records must remain pristine, confidential, and compliant with health information privacy standards. Modifying the clinic's core intake form or permitting marketing write-access was completely out of the question.

  • Board 1 (Clinical CRM): The existing New Patient Form board remains the uncontaminated source of truth. Zero marketing columns or approval checkboxes were added to the therapists' medical intake flow.
  • Board 2 (Marketing Review): A dedicated Filmable Athletes board living in the Marketing workspace that receives only sanitized, necessary patient metadata.
  • Strict One-Way Governance: Clinical data flows exclusively downstream into Marketing. Marketing possesses view-only permissions with absolute zero backflow into the clinical EHR/CRM.

DATA INTEGRITY & LIFECYCLE GROUPS

Four-Stage Athlete Lifecycle & Deterministic Field Mapping

To maintain crystal-clear visibility for the content creation team, the Marketing board is partitioned into four distinct operational sections:

  • 1. Pending Review: Automatically receives newly evaluated active patients awaiting the therapist's approval.
  • 2. Current Filmable Athletes: Holds active clinic patients who have been explicitly approved by their treating physical therapist.
  • 3. Former Filmable Athletes: Automatically archives approved athletes once their clinical status changes to "Discharged."
  • 4. Not Filmable: Holds athletes who declined media consent or were excluded due to clinical injury considerations.

EVENT-DRIVEN PROVISIONING

Deterministic Item Creation with 100% Run Success

Item creation occurs solely when an athlete meets strict criteria: Patient Status = Active (or Lead - Enter Patient) and Evaluation Date is populated.

  • Auditable Run History: Built-in run logging confirms 100% success across all executions without unhandled edge cases or payload drops.
  • Zero Mirror Overhead: Replaces heavy multi-board mirror column queries with atomic, instantaneous item generation.
  • Zero Duplicate Protection: Item creation conditions prevent re-triggering upon routine non-status column edits in the CRM.

SLACK ENGINE & IDENTITY RESOLUTION

Intelligent 24h Delayed Direct Messages & Identity Sync

Asking therapists about filming right at evaluation time is ineffective because therapists need time to establish patient rapport and evaluate rehabilitation compliance.

  • 24-Hour Evaluation Delay: Uses Zapier Formatter (Date/Time) and Delay Until to schedule the notification exactly 24 hours post-initial evaluation.
  • Google Sheets Identity Lookup: Queries the central clinic directory to map Monday.com user IDs to verified Slack workspace emails, ensuring private Direct Message delivery.
  • Workspace Automation Persona: Notifications are delivered via a branded clinic bot with interactive decision buttons rather than individual personal accounts.
View Delayed Slack Pipeline
AUTOMATED RECURRING LOGIC

Daily Reminder Looping & Instant Decision Termination

To prevent approvals from stalling in busy clinic schedules, a resilient daily scheduled loop re-checks pending athletes without flooding the clinic with redundant noise.

  • Automated 24h Schedule Trigger: Every 24 hours, the scheduler triggers, searches for all items with status "Pending Review," and constructs a line-item loop.
  • Condition Re-Verification: Filters confirm each item remains pending before querying Google Sheets for the assigned therapist's Slack handle.
  • Instant Loop Break: The exact moment a therapist clicks "Yes" or "No" in Slack, the status updates in Monday.com, immediately excluding the patient from future reminder loops.

REAL-TIME LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT

Automated Discharged Status Sync & Roster Re-Routing

One of the critical operational vulnerabilities in sports medicine marketing is filming patients who are no longer actively treated at the clinic. Petroski Physio completely automated this lifecycle transition.

  • CRM Column Value Interception: When a therapist marks a patient as "Discharged" in the Clinical CRM, a real-time webhook immediately catches the event.
  • Deterministic Filtering: Inspects the old and new column values to ensure only true discharges proceed to the routing step.
  • Automatic Group Relocation: The Zapier pipeline automatically moves the patient's record from "Current Filmable Athletes" to "Former Filmable Athletes", preserving real-time marketing roster accuracy and brand safety.
