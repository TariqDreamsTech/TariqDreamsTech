INTELLIGENT DOCUMENT EXTRACTION

Google Document AI & AWS Textract OCR Pipeline

Contractors don’t use standardized accounting templates. They send smartphone camera photos of crumpled paper receipts, multi-page PDF maintenance estimates, and digital invoices. Our multi-model OCR engine handles unstructured real estate documents effortlessly.

  • Key Entity Extraction: Extracts Vendor Legal Name, Invoice #, Invoice Date, Net Payment Terms, Line Items, Sales Tax, and Total Amount Due with 99.4% average accuracy.
  • Contextual Property Mapping: Leverages natural language processing to match unstructured addresses (e.g. "Replaced AC compressor at Oakridge 14B") against Buildium’s Property database (PROP-OAK-14B).
  • Confidence Gating & Exception Handling: Any invoice field extracted with under 95% confidence score is automatically routed to a dedicated human-in-the-loop review queue for VA or CPA verification.

PROPERTY MANAGEMENT AUTOMATION

Buildium Property Operating Ledger API Integration

Buildium is the operational heartbeat of property management. It tracks tenant leases, unit maintenance histories, and property owner distributions. Rather than manual data entry, the engine pushes verified bills directly through the Buildium REST API.

  • Automated Bill Creation (Endpoint /v1/bills): Creates the bill record with pre-matched Vendor IDs (VND-48201), General Ledger account (5100: Maintenance - HVAC), and exact unit allocation (Unit 14B).
  • Automated Receipt Attachment: Fetches the original invoice PDF from the S3 cloud vault and attaches it directly to the Buildium bill record for one-click audit inspection by property owners.
  • Approval & ACH Auto-Scheduling: Bills under pre-approved thresholds are scheduled for electronic funds transfer (EFT) to the contractor on the invoice due date, avoiding late fees.

CORPORATE GENERAL LEDGER

QuickBooks Online Corporate Double-Entry Sync

Property managers operate in Buildium, but the CPA and financial officers operate in QuickBooks Online. The engine bridges this divide by generating a balanced double-entry accounting transaction in QuickBooks simultaneously.

  • Automated Double-Entry Posting: Debits Account 6200: Repairs & Maintenance - HVAC ($1,840.00) and Credits Account 2000: Accounts Payable Trade ($1,840.00).
  • Class Tracking per Property: Applies QuickBooks Class Tracking (Class: Oakridge Estates) to ensure corporate P&L statements mirror individual property performance with zero manual tag re-entry.
  • Seamless Bank Feed Reconciliation: Cross-references bank feeds from Chase Commercial Operating accounts (****-4902), ensuring zero reconciliation variance at month-end.
