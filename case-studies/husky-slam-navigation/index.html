Kinematic Formulation

Differential-Drive Odometry Calibration & Wheel Slip Suppression

Clearpath Husky relies on a 4-wheel skid-steer mechanical design. Approximating skid-steering through differential-drive kinematics introduces systematic odometry errors due to track slip during rotational turns. By precisely measuring and tuning the effective wheel separation ( = 1.07025 ext{ m}$) and wheel radius ( = 0.1651 ext{ m}$), odometry drift was minimized prior to sensor fusion.

control.yaml — Husky Differential Odometry Config YAML 
husky_velocity_controller:
  type: "diff_drive_controller/DiffDriveController"
  left_wheel: ['front_left_wheel', 'rear_left_wheel']
  right_wheel: ['front_right_wheel', 'rear_right_wheel']
  publish_rate: 50.0
  pose_covariance_diagonal: [0.001, 0.001, 1000000.0, 1000000.0, 1000000.0, 0.03]
  twist_covariance_diagonal: [0.001, 0.001, 0.001, 1000000.0, 1000000.0, 0.03]
  cmd_vel_timeout: 0.25

  # Kinematic calibration parameters extracted from physical verification
  wheel_separation: 1.07025
  wheel_radius: 0.1651

  # Odometry slip compensation factors
  wheel_separation_multiplier: 1.875
  wheel_radius_multiplier: 1.000

  # Velocity Limits for stable non-skid traversal
  linear:
    x:
      has_velocity_limits: true
      max_velocity: 1.0   # m/s
      has_acceleration_limits: true
      max_acceleration: 1.5 # m/s^2

      has_velocity_limits: true

Mapping & Localization

Rao-Blackwellized Particle Filtering (Gmapping SLAM)

Traditional EKF-SLAM scales quadratically (N^2)$ with the number of environmental features. In this system, Ahsan Tariq deployed the Gmapping algorithm—leveraging a Rao-Blackwellized particle filter where each particle carries an individual map hypothesis: 77\mathcal{P}(x_{1:t}, m \mid z_{1:t}, u_{1:t-1}) = \mathcal{P}(m \mid x_{1:t}, z_{1:t}) \cdot \mathcal{P}(x_{1:t} \mid z_{1:t}, u_{1:t-1})77 By factoring the trajectory estimation from the grid mapping, particle depletion was prevented, and real-time loop closures were achieved when re-visiting previously traversed corridors.

Adaptive Resampling & Scan Matching

Calculates matching probability based on scan geometry rather than purely dead-reckoning odometry. Resampling triggers dynamically based on the effective sample size ({ ext{eff}}$), keeping CPU load under 28% on an onboard Jetson Xavier / Intel i7.

Deterministic Loop Closure

Correlates current planar laser sweeps against historical grid submaps. Upon loop detection, all particle weights update globally, eliminating cumulative angular drift and snapping wall boundaries into sharp 90-degree alignment.

Trajectory Execution

Global Dijkstra Routing & Dynamic Window Local Obstacle Avoidance

Autonomous navigation requires two concurrent planners operating at different time scales: a Global Planner calculating topological clearance across the static map, and a Local Planner reacting to unexpected dynamic obstacles in real time.

Dijkstra Global Cost Field

Computes the minimum-cost path from the robot's current pose to the user-selected 2D Nav Goal on an inflated potential field. Cost decay ensures the path maintains a safe standoff distance from concrete walls.

DWA Local Trajectory Sampling

Samples velocities within the allowable kinematic window $ every 0 ext{ ms}$. Evaluates circular arcs against the local rolling costmap and selects the trajectory that maximizes forward speed while guaranteeing zero collision.

Operational Transformation

Before vs After: Manual Teleop vs Autonomous ROS SLAM

A clear comparison between conventional manual remote-control driving and Ahsan Tariq's fully autonomous SLAM & path-planning architecture.

BEFORE (BLIND MANUAL CONTROL)

Zero Map Knowledge & Collision Susceptibility

Operators had to pilot the robot using low-latency video transmitters. Without digital map coordinates or obstacle distance sensors, blind spots led to repeated chassis collisions with columns and walls.

AFTER (AUTONOMOUS GMAPPING SLAM)

Sub-2cm Real-Time Occupancy Grid Generation

Planar LiDAR sweeps generate a millimetric 2D map in real time. Rao-Blackwellized particle filtering closes loops automatically, providing true situational awareness with zero human telemetry guesswork.

BEFORE (UNCOMPENSATED DRIFT)

Catastrophic Skid-Steer Odometry Accumulation

4-wheel skid-steering introduced severe rotational wheel slippage. After just three 90-degree turns, uncalibrated dead-reckoning odometry drifted by over 35 degrees, ruining downstream coordinate frames.

AFTER (KINEMATIC MULTIPLIER TUNING)

Empirical Calibration & Laser Scan Correlation

Precisely tuned effective track separation (.07025 ext{ m}$) with an empirical multiplier of .875$. Coupled with laser scan matching, positional drift is bounded to $< 1.2\%$ over extensive mission routes.

BEFORE (CONSTANT OPERATOR FATIGUE)

Full-Time Joystick Micromanagement

Navigating complex corridors required constant manual steering inputs, causing extreme cognitive fatigue during long search-and-rescue or industrial facility inspection runs.

AFTER (1-CLICK 2D NAV GOAL)

Autonomous Dijkstra Planning & Dynamic Obstacle Clearance

Operators simply click a goal waypoint on the RViz map. The global planner finds the optimal route instantly, and the DWA local planner steers smoothly around unexpected obstacles without human intervention.
