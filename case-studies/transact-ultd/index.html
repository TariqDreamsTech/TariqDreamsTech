How Transact ULTD Replaced Sales Friction with Autonomous AI

See how traditional merchant services phone tag was transformed into a sub-second autonomous pipeline that qualifies leads, forwards calls to specialists, and delivers rate comparisons instantly.

The Old Manual Way (Before)

4-Hour Lead Callback Lag & Missed Merchants

Inbound leads from referrals and online forms waited in email inboxes for hours. Sales reps played phone tag while busy business owners were on the road. By the time a human rep followed up, the merchant had already signed with a competitor.

The Autonomous Voice Engine (After)

Sub-60s Inbound/Outbound AI Voice Qualification

Retell AI engages merchants within 60 seconds with natural sub-second voice latency. It introduces Transact ULTD, asks for monthly volume ($180k/mo), extracts owner names, and verifies intent conversationally without human intervention.

The Old Manual Way (Before)

Manual Hold Times & Disconnected Transfers

When a merchant asked a specific question about POS terminals, high-risk compliance, or custom interchange rates, reps put them on long holds, dialed extension numbers manually, and frequently dropped the call, losing high-value accounts.

The Autonomous Voice Engine (After)

Twilio Elastic SIP & 5-Specialist Smart Forwarding

Custom Twilio SIP Trunking monitors live conversation intents. If a merchant needs human escalation, the engine bridges the call dynamically via TwiML <Dial><Sip> to one of 5 specialized team members (Rates, Terminals, Underwriting, Senior AE, Tech Support).

The Old Manual Way (Before)

Human Typing Typos & Missing CRM Records

Staff manually entered contact names, emails, and phone numbers into Iris CRM. Key details from phone calls were forgotten, follow-up emails were delayed for days, and leads lacked structured assignment or audit trails.

The Autonomous Voice Engine (After)

Instant Iris CRM API Provisioning & Sticky Notes

The engine performs two-step verification, creates the lead in Iris CRM, parses first/last names, sets Status 104 (Group 1), assigns Lead ID 35 (Tony), dispatches Email Template 384, pins automated sticky notes, and syncs to Google Sheets in 3 seconds.

The Old Manual Way (Before)

3 to 5 Hours on Spreadsheets per Statement

Calculating interchange fees, tiered qualified markups, mid-qual, non-qual surcharges, and basis point savings from a 15-page First Data or Fiserv statement required hours of tedious spreadsheet math by an experienced financial analyst.

The Autonomous Voice Engine (After)

Automated ISO AMP Side-by-Side Savings Proposal

Uploaded statements trigger an automated ISO AMP webhook that parses line items, calculates true interchange pass-through savings ($1,383/mo), generates a professional side-by-side comparison PDF, and emails it to the client automatically.

The Old Manual Way (Before)

Siloed Processors & Repeated Inquiries

Operations teams lacked visibility into which step a lead was stuck in. Merchants called repeatedly to ask if their statements were received, whether their hardware was being shipped, or if their rate proposal was ready.

The Autonomous Voice Engine (After)

Centralized Event Bus & Automated Client Notifications

Digital signature triggers, hardware task creation, setup scheduling, and status updates trigger automated notifications across Iris CRM, SendGrid, and AT&T business lines with complete end-to-end transparency.

CONVERSATIONAL INTELLIGENCE & VALIDATION

Retell AI Voice Qualification & Two-Step Security Gate

Rather than sending inbound leads to voicemail or clunky keypad IVR menus, Transact ULTD uses an autonomous Retell AI conversational voice agent. Operating with sub-second speech latency and natural speech pacing, the agent engages business owners in fluent, human dialogue.

  • Natural Intent & Entity Extraction: Dynamically extracts company legal name, decision maker’s first and last name, business email address, phone number, and current monthly processing volume.
  • Two-Step Cryptographic Verification Check: When the voice call terminates, a secure POST webhook fires. The payload is checked against strict verification parameters (two_step_verification = true). If verification fails, the pipeline halts with a clean error, preventing spam or junk entries from entering the CRM.
  • Real-Time Call Transcript Storage: The entire conversational transcript, customer sentiment, and duration are preserved and formatted for immediate executive visibility.

ADVANCED TELEPHONY & SMART CALL TRANSFER

Twilio Elastic SIP Trunking & Dynamic 5-Specialist Live Forwarding

A critical breakthrough in this architecture is the seamless bridging between artificial intelligence and human expertise. If a merchant asks a specialized technical or rate question during the AI call, the system executes an instant warm call transfer via Twilio Elastic SIP Trunking.

  • Twilio Elastic SIP Infrastructure: Configured dedicated Twilio termination endpoints (transactultd.pstn.twilio.com) with G.711u codecs, TLS v1.3 signaling encryption, and active SRTP audio streams.
  • Intent-Based 5-Specialist Routing: The engine matches the merchant’s specific inquiry against 5 specialized team members:

CRM INTEGRATION & DATA PROVISIONING

Deep IRIS CRM Integration: Automated Provisioning & Sticky Notes

IRIS CRM is the premier operating system for US merchant acquiring ISOs. Rather than relying on staff to manually transcribe phone calls, the engine communicates directly with the IRIS CRM REST API (transactultd.iriscrm.com) to execute immediate, error-free lead provisioning.

  • Automated Lead Generation: Creates new merchant records with clean parsed parameters (legal business name, DBA, principal owner first and last name, business email, and verified phone).
  • Automated Assignment to User 35: Directly assigns the incoming lead to Tony (User ID 35 · tony@transactultd.com) and updates the lead status code to 104 (Group 1: "Statement Needed").
  • Automated Transactional Email (Template 384): Dispatches an automated branded email to the merchant’s verified address with a secure link to upload their 2–3 recent credit card processing statements.
  • Pinned Sticky Notes & Team Alerts: Attaches an automated sticky note ("Statement Needed Email Sent") to the top of the merchant’s file, notifying all assigned internal stakeholders.

FINANCIAL ANALYSIS & SAVINGS AUTOMATION

Automated ISO AMP Statement Analysis & Side-by-Side Proposal Engine

In merchant acquiring, closing deals depends entirely on demonstrating undeniable mathematical savings. Previously, calculating interchange plus savings from complex 15-page First Data or Fiserv statements took 3 to 5 hours. We engineered an end-to-end automated ISO AMP webhook pipeline.

  • Automated Statement Parsing (Complete 2): When merchant statements are uploaded, the engine normalizes transaction volumes, batch fees, and effective rates into the ISO AMP analysis environment.
  • Automated Side-by-Side Generation (Complete 3): Receives ISO AMP calculation webhooks, performs line-item comparison between the merchant’s current tiered rate (e.g., 2.89% effective) and Transact ULTD’s wholesale Interchange Plus rate (2.14% effective).
  • Instant Commercial Value Delivery: Automatically compiles a side-by-side proposal proving net savings (e.g. $1,383.90/month, or $16,606.80/year), updates the lead status in Iris CRM to "Analysis Sent", and emails the comparison report directly to the merchant.

Engineering Transparency: Third-Party Processor Integration Bottlenecks

In enterprise integration, true architectural leadership requires documenting external platform limitations candidly. While all internal intake, telephony, verification, CRM, and ISO AMP automations were built and published successfully, direct automated underwriting submission into third-party payment processors remains constrained by processor portal limitations:

iAccess Portal
403 Forbidden Access
The iAccess portal returns a hard 403 Forbidden error for automated API requests, preventing programmatic onboarding review without elevated partner whitelist access.
InStore Processor
Missing API Documentation
While the login portal is accessible, the processor provides zero standardized API specifications or developer sandbox, making direct code automation unsafe.
PayBright Platform
Partner-Only Restriction
Available integration documentation strictly supports pre-negotiated partnered ecosystems, lacking standalone public endpoints for third-party ingestion.
