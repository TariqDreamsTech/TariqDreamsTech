DATA PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE

Eliminating NetSuite API Bottlenecks With a 20-Stream Local PostgreSQL Mirror

NetSuite's standard SuiteTalk and REST APIs suffered from strict concurrency limits and sluggish multi-second latency. To power real-time dashboards for hundreds of healthcare executives simultaneously without crashing ERP bandwidth, we architected a continuous 20-stream bidirectional ETL synchronization engine.

  • 20 Concurrent Data Streams: High-frequency transactions sync every 2 minutes; master catalogs and organizational hierarchies refresh every 2 hours.
  • Zero NetSuite Concurrency Blocks: Local Cloud SQL PostgreSQL database serves all portal reads in <15ms, completely isolating the ERP from traffic spikes.
  • Massive Record Volume: Mirrors 51,462 Invoices, 35,935 Sales Orders, 24,634 Purchase Orders, 5,173 Suppliers, 5,063 SKUs, and 740 PeoplesHR employee records flawlessly.

ENTERPRISE SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

Bridging On-Premise Physical Servers, Azure SharePoint & 3-Layer OTP Vault

Ambica stored sensitive regulatory files (FDA CPR filings, formulation chemistry, executive contracts) on isolated physical on-premise servers and Microsoft SharePoint. We engineered a hybrid edge bridge combining bank-grade Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), 3-layer OTP authentication, and forensic audit logging.

  • 79 Roles & 750 Permission Scopes: 9,400 role links mirroring NetSuite user provisioning with instant global session revocation across all connected devices.
  • Secure Physical Server Bridge: Lightweight daemon running on on-premise hardware that streams confidential documents to authenticated cloud users with zero port forwarding or firewall exposure.
  • Forensic Audit Logging (1,246+ Entries): Immutable ledger capturing every write event, OTP generation, login, logout, and document inspection (e.g. LEGAL_WORKING_DOCUMENT_VIEWED) with IP and timestamp verification.

The Transformation: Before vs After Dovetail

A simple, human-readable comparison of the agonizing manual paper delays versus the autonomous digital speed engineered by Ahsan Tariq.

BEFORE (4–7 DAY PAPER STALLS)

Life-Saving Medicines Trapped Waiting For Signatures

An urgent shipment of medicine was packed and ready on Monday. But because the director was visiting a distant regional branch, the physical paper clipboard couldn't get signed. The box sat on a warehouse floor for 5 days while patients waited.

AFTER (<60 SECONDS MOBILE APPROVAL)

Instant 1-Click Digital Signatures Anywhere

The moment the warehouse packs the medicine, the director receives a secure push alert on their phone. With one quick tap on DocuSign, the approval is verified in 45 seconds, and the delivery bike leaves immediately.

BEFORE (BLIND ROADWAY TRANSIT)

Zero Idea Where 102 Delivery Vehicles Were

When a major hospital called asking, “Where is our insulin shipment?”, staff had to make 10 frantic phone calls to drivers who were stuck in traffic or couldn't answer their phones. Delays were completely blind.

AFTER (100% LIVE CARTRACK GPS RADAR)

Real-Time Vehicle Speeds & Minute-Accurate Arrival

The live radar shows all 102 delivery bikes, vans, and trucks on a live map. Dispatchers see exact speeds (e.g. 42 km/h), vehicle battery/fuel status, driver safety scores, and can tell the hospital the exact minute of arrival.

BEFORE (LOCKED IN AN IT ROOM)

Files Trapped on Physical Office Hard Drives

Vital government health certifications (FDA CPRs) and supplier contracts were stored on old physical desktop servers inside a locked central IT room. If an inspector asked for proof at 8 PM, someone had to drive to the office in traffic.

AFTER (ZERO-TRUST 3-LAYER OTP CLOUD VAULT)

Secure 1-Time Code Access from Anywhere

Authorized executives can safely view confidential compliance files from home or on the road using a 3-layer one-time password (OTP) sent to their phone, securely streaming files from physical servers with zero hacking risk.

BEFORE (15-SECOND SPINNING WHEELS)

NetSuite Freezes & "Rate Limit Exceeded" Errors

Every time an accountant tried to open a financial report during month-end closing, NetSuite froze or threw rate limit errors because 100 people were clicking at once. Morning dispatch was delayed by hours.

AFTER (20-STREAM CONTINUOUS LOCAL MIRROR)

51,462 Invoices Mirrored in <15ms Response

A custom background engine synchronizes 51,462 invoices and 35,935 sales orders continuously. Reports open instantly in under 15 milliseconds without touching NetSuite's bandwidth or causing a single slowdown.

BEFORE (MILLIONS IN FRAGMENTED SPREADSHEETS)

Managing $140M in Freight on Scattered Excel Files

Over $140 Million worth of international pharmaceutical shipments flying across air cargo and container ships were tracked across disorganized personal Excel sheets. Different departments had different numbers.

AFTER ($141.3M LIVE TRELLO PIPELINE RADAR)

Unified Global Shipment Radar With Live Alerts

1,466 global shipments ($141.3M active value) are tracked with automated milestone cards and real-time status updates from international port dispatch to hospital delivery.
