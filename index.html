On factory production lines, defective items are rare, meaning AI systems must learn to detect anomalies without ever seeing damaged training examples. In this rigorous computer vision research on the world-standard MVTec AD benchmark, I tested three major AI paradigms: web-trained vision models (CLIP), deep supervised neural networks, and self-reconstructing autoencoders. The study uncovered critical industry blind spots — including standard networks cheating with fake 92% scores on empty images — and proved that local memory banks of normal parts are the true winning architecture.

The AI detects cyber attacks with 98.67% accuracy in less than a millisecond, and exposes key insights on how to catch rare, stealthy computer worms.
