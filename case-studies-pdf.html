#21 Robust Federated Learning — Defense Against Byzantine Model Poisoning
Academic Research
Client / Organization: Peer-Reviewed Research · Sole Author: Ahsan Tariq
Timeline: 2025 – 2026
Domain: Decentralized AI · Cybersecurity Defense
⚠️ The Painful Problem Faced

Hospitals or fleets of autonomous vehicles need to train shared AI models together without exposing sensitive patient medical records or vehicle GPS coordinates to central servers. However, if a rogue or hacked device submits corrupted data, it can poison and crash the shared AI brain.

💡 The Smart Solution Built

Engineered a mathematical 'Trust Gate' algorithm that inspects AI model updates from every participant before accepting them into the global network. It detects subtle mathematical misalignments, instantly blocking and rejecting malicious updates without needing to look at private raw data.

🚀 The Real-World Business Results Delivered

Maintained 99.1% AI model accuracy even under coordinated cyber sabotage where standard federated learning failed completely, paving the way for safe, privacy-preserving AI collaboration across hospitals and connected vehicles.
